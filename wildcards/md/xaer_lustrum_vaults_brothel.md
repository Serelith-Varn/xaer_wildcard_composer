## 1. Spatial Architecture & Environmental Design of the Lustrum Vaults

The **Lustrum Vaults**—known colloquially as the **Silk Alleys**—represent the subterranean pleasure and hospitality district of Xaer'Thalassor, operated under the charter of **House Mirella**. Located beneath the secondary market tiers of the Central Basin, the district is carved directly out of porous, heat-retaining black basalt.

### 🏛️ Architectural Taxonomy & Ambient Features

* **Subterranean Basalt Formations:** Chambers and alcoves are excavated into vaulted, organic arches of volcanic black stone, polished to a liquid sheen along high-traffic corridors and left rough-hewn along private divan niches.
* **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Illumination is provided by low-hanging brass censers and blown-glass lanterns burning bioluminescent purple oil and fragrant tallow. The light casts deep, warm violet and amber shadows across the stone surfaces, illuminating the mineral steam that rises continuously from floor flues.
* **Drapery & Fabrics:** In accordance with *House Mnemos Code 88-K*, opaque partitions are prohibited. Private alcoves and lounging areas are partitioned using sheer, translucent crimson and midnight-violet gossamer silks. These fabrics hang from brass ceiling trusses and are anchored at the floor by heavy, polished moonsteel weight rings.
* **Furnishings & Comfort:** Central reception halls feature broad, low-slung divans carved from petrified cedar and black basalt, heaped with plush velvet cushions, bearskin pelts, and raw-silk throws. Heat-pads connected to subterranean sulfur conduits warm the seating surfaces to a comfortable, skin-soothing temperature.
* **Acoustic & Olfactory Profile:** The porous basalt walls absorb harsh echoes, deadening high-pitched noise while allowing soft whispers, chiming anklets, and water-flute melodies to resonate smoothly. The air is warm, humid, and scented with dried lotus pollen, sweet almond oil, roasted chicory, and smoldering myrrh.

## 2. Legal Status & Somatic Jurisprudence of the Houses of Welcome

In Xaer'Thalassor, brothels and pleasure chambers are not illicit dens of vice; they are legally recognized **Houses of Welcome**—sacred institutions dedicated to somatic hospitality, emotional tuning, psychotherapeutic healing, and diplomatic mediation.

### 📜 Canonical Principles & Statutory Codes

1. **Default Civic Nudity (House Mnemos Code 88-K):** Courtesans and visitors move unclad under default civic law. Garments exist purely as sheer translucent crimson or violet silks, anchored directly to earned body piercings (nipple studs, navel rings, collarbone anchors).
2. **The Sanctity of the Hearth-Bed:** Coercion, physical violence, or the use of intoxicating mind-poisons within a registered House of Welcome carries the immediate penalty of public enslavement or death. Clients visit the Lustrum Vaults to have the harsh psychic static of court life massaged away by attuned companions.
3. **The Redemption Ledger:** By civic law, a portion of every transaction in a House of Welcome is deposited directly into the companion's personal *Redemption Ledger*. After a set cycle of service, a brothel-ward possesses the unconditional right to purchase her total freedom, retire as an honored guild elder, or transition into a private House partnership.
4. **Lex Closure (The Intimate Seal):** All formal agreements, financial settlements, and diplomatic reconciliations concluded within the Silk Alleys are sealed via an intimate somatic act (*Lex Closure*), aligning the nervous systems of both parties with the living Echo.

### Scene A: The Reception in the Silk Alleys
* **Setting:** The grand reception hall of the Lustrum Vaults beneath the Central Basin.
* **Narrative Overview:** High-caste noblewomen and off-duty fleet officers recline upon heated basalt divans, greeted by House Mirella courtesans. Soft purple oil lanterns filter through translucent crimson drapes while attendants pour hot lumenroot wine into obsidian chalices.

* **Setting:** The subterranean scullery beneath *The Weeping Anchor* in the Lower Docks.
* **Narrative Overview:** Before her ascension as the Silken Chain, Syla ("Coffee") served as a brothel drudge in the damp, chicory-scented scullery of Madame Vendra, taking punishment for young novices like Mirella before being claimed as Lot Zero by Flamekeeper Aestra.
